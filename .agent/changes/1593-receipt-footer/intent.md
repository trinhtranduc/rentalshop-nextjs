# Bill footer says the bill was made with AnyRent

Issue: #1593 · Author: Trinh Tran (owner) · Status: in-progress · Created: 2026-11-15

## Problem

The last line of every printed bill says "Tải AnyRent trên App Store" / "Download AnyRent on App Store":
the shop web slip (`apps/client/app/orders/receipt/ReceiptSlip.tsx`), the iOS thermal print (`Order.swift`) and
the Android thermal print (`ThermalPrinter.kt`, hard-coded English, and it names the App Store on Android).
The owner wants the line to say the bill was made with the AnyRent app.

## Proposed outcome

The three bills end with "Hóa đơn được tạo bằng ứng dụng AnyRent" in Vietnamese. Other languages use the
string the apps already have for the shared bill image ("Receipt generated by AnyRent software", iOS
`PreviewViewController.swift:1728`, Android `receipt_generated_by`), translated in 12 languages.

## Affected users and systems

Shops printing bills from the web (client), iOS and Android. No API, no data.

## Constraints

- Thermal printers print without accents (`formatStringOriginalCharacter`, `PrintText.plain`).
- Installed apps keep the old line until they update.
- Simple: reuse the existing string; no new translations except the web key.

## Open questions

- None. Wording: owner asked "Bill được sinh ra từ ứng dụng AnyRent" or a recommendation.

## Decision log

- 2026-11-15 — "Hóa đơn được tạo bằng ứng dụng AnyRent": "Hóa đơn" is the word on the bill header and in the
  apps; "tạo bằng" reads more naturally than "sinh ra từ". The Vietnamese text of the shared-image string
  changes to the same sentence, so image and print agree (Claude, on the owner's "bạn recommend giúp tôi").
